01 · About

I’m curious about how things work, then I want to build my own version.

That is what pulled me toward Computer Engineering. I like being able to follow a system from a physical signal or circuit, through the code, and all the way to the person using it. At York, that has meant studying embedded systems and computer organization while building software, RF prototypes, and technology for community events.

Fitness has been a constant for me for more than four years. I train consistently, stay on top of my nutrition, and have learned that progress comes from doing the small things well, even when motivation is low. Becoming a certified personal trainer let me pair that discipline with a deeper understanding of strength training, program design, and how to help other people make progress too.
