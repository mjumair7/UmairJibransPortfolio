01 · About

I learn best by building, breaking, and improving things.

If I am learning a protocol, I want to wire it up, send real messages through it, break it on purpose, and see how the system recovers. That is how the CAN-FD test bench came together, and it is the same mindset I bring to websites, data projects, and tools used by community teams.

Outside engineering, the gym is where I reset. I have trained consistently for more than four years and became a certified personal trainer because I wanted to understand the work at a deeper level. Coaching has also made me a better teammate: explain things clearly, meet people where they are, and adjust the plan when it is not working.

CAN-FD ECU Test Bench

I built a two-controller STM32 test bench where a sensor ECU publishes temperature and RPM frames and an actuator ECU checks message integrity and freshness before controlling a fan. A Python validation station captures traffic, injects dropped or delayed frames, and verifies timeout handling, fail-safe behaviour, and recovery.
